# Awesome LinkedIn Ads Manager

*Unofficial community list for LinkedIn Ads Manager (Campaign Manager). Not affiliated with LinkedIn. All trademarks belong to their owners.*

A curated list for anyone working in linkedin ads manager - officially LinkedIn Campaign Manager, the platform for creating, measuring and optimising LinkedIn ad campaigns in one place. Every link is a page LinkedIn's own advertising site links from its navigation, grouped the way a media buyer needs them: sign in, learn, choose a format, check specs, measure. Third-party tools are limited to what the sources name.

> Want to see the ads your competitors already run before you brief your own? [Try Reachara - competitor ad monitoring and creator discovery](https://reachara.com?utm_source=github&utm_medium=ugc&utm_campaign=awesome-linkedin-ads-manager&utm_content=readme-top&utm_term=tier-r).
